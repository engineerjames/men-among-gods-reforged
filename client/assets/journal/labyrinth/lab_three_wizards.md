**Lab Three: Wizards**

**Overview**

Spiritwalkers have a bad reputation, and groups like the one in this labyrinth are the reason why. This sect of Harakim were once a powerful force in Aston until Gorn, the demigod of the Harakim, cursed them to the Labyrinth. They wander the halls, in constant remembrance of their wicked deeds and amnesia to all other knowledge.

```spoiler
The harakim are all strong, but their 3 leaders especially so. Killing them may reveal a powerful boon to leaving the Labyrinth.
```

```spoiler
Some doors don't open with keys, but may require tools to open instead. How many tools you may need, who knows…
```

```spoiler
The halls are long, but you'll need to explore to the very end to complete the Labyrinth.
```
