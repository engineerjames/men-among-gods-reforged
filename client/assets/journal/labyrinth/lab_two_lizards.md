**Lab Two: Lizards**

**Overview**

If Grolms do not coordinate, then Lizards are their opposite: cunning, ruthless magic users who have a structured leadership and specific aims. If you're to leave this Labyrinth, you must coordinate like your enemies. Are there any allies in this lab, who you could assist to move forward? Worthy of consideration…
