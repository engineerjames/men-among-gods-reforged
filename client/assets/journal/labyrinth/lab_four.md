**Lab Four**

**Overview**

The Labyrinths are meant as a test, but contain very real examples that all adventurers can learn from. Unfortunately at times there are…repeat lessons. A lust for power gone wrong, being one. The Harakim & Templar in this dungeon left beliefs in their deities. This is not enough to make the gods angry. No. What angered them, was their cultist attempts to then claim the power of the gods for their own.

```spoiler
Consider the “Big Picture” in this Labyrinth. Things are not as they may appear at first.
```

```spoiler
Don't let the enemies here pull the rug out from under you. There may just be something underneath it.
```
