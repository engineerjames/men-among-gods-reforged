**Lab One: Grolms**

**Overview**

The Grolms are a violent race. They will work together to kill anything that is not a Grolm, but their sense of community ends there. However, we've found some that seem oddly coordinated. They have leadership: a mage, and a king they've declared the head of their little group. Are you able to find the 3 keys after defeating them to move forward to the 2nd Labyrinth?

Understand, these Labyrinths are a test of many things. Your wits, strength, and capabilities to achieve a greater power than ever before.

